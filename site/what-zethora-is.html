What Zethora is

Zethora is private, self-custodied money that ordinary people can mine.

Nobody gets coins without mining. There is no premine, no sale, no presale, no airdrop, and there never will be. Every coin in existence was made by someone’s CPU doing the work.

The code is the truth. The paper is the map.

We publish what is unfinished, because trust comes before growth. You should know what is not done before you spend any time on Zethora.

The promise

  • 100 million ZTHR, hard cap, enforced by consensus. Nobody can mint more.
  • No premine. Not for the team, not for the founder, not for anyone.
  • No token sale. There is nothing to buy. ZTHR is only created by mining.
  • Privacy stays off at mainnet launch, and turns on only after an independent review of the private pool, at a height announced in advance.
  • No price, no return, no passive income. This is not an investment. Testnet coins are worth zero.
  • No launch dates. It ships when it is done and audited.

Why it exists

Bitcoin showed that scarcity without an owner is possible. Kaspa showed that a BlockDAG at high block rate works in production. Zcash showed that strong privacy can be built on real cryptography. Monero showed that CPU mining is viable at scale.

Zethora takes those ideas and adds the integration: Orchard privacy inside GHOSTDAG, with a turnstile that tracks the supply flowing into and out of the private pool. That integration is the work.

Who it belongs to

Zethora is not owned by a company. It is not a product with customers. It belongs to whoever runs it: the miners who secure it, the people who hold it, and the people who build on it. Decisions are made in the open. The rules that protect you, the cap, the privacy gate, the no-premine rule, are not up for a vote. Everything else is yours to shape.

How to check us

Do not take our word for anything on this page. The code is public. The supply check runs on every block and shows BALANCED when every coin is accounted for. If it ever does not, stop and tell us. The status page lists what is unfinished. Read it before you trust us.
