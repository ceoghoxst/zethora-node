Zethora Network Status

What is Zethora?
A Layer-1 Proof of Work blockchain using a BlockDAG architecture, RandomZ CPU mining, and Zcash Orchard privacy.

Is there a token or presale?
No. There is no token, no presale, and no premine.

Are smart contracts built?
No. Programmability is future research, not built.

Is the privacy layer audited?
No. Private payments are switched off on testnet and mainnet until an outside cryptographer reviews the privacy layer.

Can I mine it?
Yes, it is home-mineable via RandomZ CPU mining. However, public testnet is not open yet.

What are testnet coins worth?
Testnet coins are worth zero.
