Public Testnet · Faucet · 1 ZTHR per claim

Zethora Testnet Faucet.

Testnet coins are worth zero. They exist so you can try a payment or see a balance without mining first.

Find your address with zethora-wallet receive --testnet. One claim per address per hour.

How it works

  • Paste your address — it starts with zethoratest:, and the checksum is checked before anything is sent.
  • Press Claim — the faucet sends exactly 1 ZTHR from its own wallet to yours.
  • Keep the transaction id — it shows up in your balance after one confirmation, a few seconds on testnet.
The faucet needs a synced testnet node and a funded faucet wallet to work. Testnet coins are worth nothing and there is no presale and no airdrop. GitHub · Getting started
